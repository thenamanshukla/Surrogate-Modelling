# Gaussian Processes, Kernels & Surrogate Modeling Resources

Beyond the primary textbook, Gaussian Processes for Machine Learning by Rasmussen and Williams, a wide range of other sources were used throughout this internship. The most useful of them are collected below, organised by topic.

## Interactive Tutorials
- [Gaussian Processes Regression Tutorial (Google Colab)](https://colab.research.google.com/github/jwangjie/Gaussian-Processes-Regression-Tutorial/blob/master/gpr_tutorial.ipynb): *Jie Wang, hands-on GPR notebook*

## Gaussian Process Fundamentals
- [GP Tutorial & Examples](https://github.com/neelsoumya/gaussian_process_tutorial): *Soumya Banerjee, University of Cambridge*
- [4F13 Lecture Notes](https://mlg.eng.cam.ac.uk/teaching/4f13/1213/lect0304.pdf): *Machine Learning Group, University of Cambridge*

## Covariance (Kernel) Functions
- [Covariance Functions & Hyperparameter Optimization](https://mlatcl.github.io/gpss/lectures/03-covariance-functions.html): *ML@CL, University of Cambridge (Neil D. Lawrence)*

## Bochner's Theorem & Kernel Approximation
- [Approximating Kernels with Random Projections](https://andrewcharlesjones.github.io/journal/bochners-theorem.html): *Andy Jones (Technical Blog)*

## Surrogate Modeling
- [More on PCE Surrogate Models](https://www.comsol.com/support/learning-center/course/surrogate-modeling-theory-271/more-on-pce-surrogate-models-97121): *COMSOL Learning Center*

## Visualization & Interactive Tools
- [VizGP Interactive GP Visualization](https://www.infinitecuriosity.org/vizgp/): *Infinite Curiosity*

## Singular Value Decomposition (SVD)
- [Singular Value Decomposition (the SVD)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mBcLRGuAFUk): *MIT OpenCourseWare (Gilbert Strang, RES.18-009 "Learn Differential Equations", Fall 2015)*

## Spectral Decomposition
- [SVD: Properties and Applications (Lecture 13)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9pNERc7Tac): *NPTEL, IIT Roorkee (Prof. S. K. Gupta & Prof. Sanjeev Kumar, "Essential Mathematics for Machine Learning")*
